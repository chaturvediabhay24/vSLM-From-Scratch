Design Decisions
Why vocab_size = 4,096?

Balances compression efficiency against model complexity. Small enough to train quickly, large enough to capture common English subwords.

Why byte-level BPE?

No UNK tokens ever — any text can be encoded as raw bytes. Language-agnostic base vocabulary of 256 byte values.

Why decoder-only transformer?

Autoregressive next-token prediction is the standard architecture for text generation (GPT-2 style). Simpler than encoder-decoder for our use case.

What can this model do?

Good at: Story openings, narrative flow, simple vocabulary, children's story patterns, grammatical correctness.

Limitations: Not ChatGPT — only 5M params vs 175B+. Context window of 128 tokens (~60 words). No factual knowledge, can't do math, code, or non-English text well. May repeat itself in long generations.
